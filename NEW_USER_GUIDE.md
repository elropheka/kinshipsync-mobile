# KinshipSync - New User Guide: From Sign-Up to Your First Event

Welcome to KinshipSync! This guide will walk you through creating your account and planning your first event step-by-step.

## 🚀 Getting Started

### Step 1: Download and Launch the App
1. Download KinshipSync from the App Store or Google Play Store
2. Open the app to see the welcome screen
3. Tap **"Sign Up"** to create your account

### Step 2: Create Your Account

#### Option A: Email Sign-Up (Recommended)
1. **Fill in Required Information:**
   - **Full Name** (required): Enter your first and last name
   - **Email Address** (required): Use a valid email you check regularly
   - **Password** (required): Create a strong password (at least 8 characters)

2. **Optional Information:**
   - **Phone Number**: Add your phone number for better communication
   - **Location**: Enter your city/country for location-based features
   - **Profile Picture**: Tap the camera icon to add a profile photo (optional but recommended)

3. **Accept Terms:**
   - Check the box to accept Terms & Conditions
   - Tap **"SIGN UP"** to create your account

#### Option B: Quick Sign-Up with Google/Apple
1. Tap **"Google"** or **"Apple"** button
2. Follow the prompts to sign in with your existing account
3. Your account will be created automatically

### Step 3: Welcome to Your Dashboard
After successful sign-up, you'll be taken to your personalized dashboard where you can:
- See upcoming events (empty for new users)
- View recent activities
- Access all app features
- Search for events

---

## 🎉 Creating Your First Event

### Step 1: Access Event Creation
1. From your dashboard, tap the **"Events"** tab at the bottom
2. Tap the **"Add new event"** button (blue button with + icon)

### Step 2: Event Basic Information
Fill in the essential details for your event:

1. **Event Name** (required)
   - Enter a clear, descriptive name
   - Example: "Smith Family Reunion 2024"

2. **Event Date** (required)
   - Tap the date field to open the date picker
   - Select your event date
   - Tap "Done" to confirm

3. **Event Time** (optional)
   - Tap the time field to set start time
   - Use the time picker to select hours and minutes

4. **Location** (optional)
   - Enter the event venue or address
   - Example: "Central Park, New York" or "123 Main St, Anytown"

5. **Description** (optional)
   - Add details about your event
   - Include what guests should expect, dress code, etc.

6. **Visibility Settings**
   - **Private**: Only invited guests can see the event
   - **Public**: Anyone can find and join the event

7. **Invite Guests** (optional)
   - Tap "Select Users" to invite specific people
   - Choose from your contacts or app users
   - You can skip this and invite people later

8. Tap **"Next"** to continue

### Step 3: Choose Event Theme
1. **Browse Available Themes**
   - Scroll through the theme options
   - Each theme has a unique color scheme and style

2. **Select Your Theme**
   - Tap on a theme to preview it
   - Choose the one that best fits your event
   - Tap **"Next"** to continue

### Step 4: Create Event Website (Optional)
1. **Website Details**
   - The app will auto-generate a website for your event
   - You can customize the content and appearance

2. **Review Website**
   - Check how your event website will look
   - Make any necessary adjustments

3. Tap **"Create Event"** to finalize

### Step 5: Event Created Successfully!
🎉 Congratulations! Your event has been created. You'll now see:
- A success message confirming your event
- Your event listed in the Events tab
- Options to manage your event

## 📱 What You Can Do Next

### Manage Your Event
1. **View Event Details**
   - Tap on your event to see full details
   - Edit information anytime

2. **Invite Guests**
   - Go to your event → "Guests" tab
   - Add guests by email or from your contacts
   - Send invitations

3. **Track RSVPs**
   - Monitor who's coming
   - Send reminders to pending guests
   - View guest preferences

4. **Plan Event Details**
   - **Tasks**: Create a to-do list for event planning
   - **Budget**: Track expenses and payments
   - **Schedule**: Plan the event timeline
   - **Seating**: Arrange seating charts
   - **Messages**: Chat with guests

### Communicate with Guests
1. **Event Messages**
   - Send announcements to all guests
   - Share photos and updates
   - Answer questions

2. **Individual Chats**
   - Start private conversations
   - Share files and documents

### Explore More Features
- **Teams**: Create family groups for ongoing collaboration
- **Vendors**: Find and book event services
- **Notifications**: Stay updated on all event activities

## 💡 Pro Tips for New Users

### Event Planning
- **Start Early**: Create events at least 2-3 weeks in advance
- **Be Descriptive**: Include all important details in your event description
- **Use Themes**: Choose themes that match your event's mood and purpose
- **Set Reminders**: Use the task feature to stay organized

### Guest Management
- **Invite Gradually**: Start with close family/friends, then expand
- **Follow Up**: Send gentle reminders for RSVPs
- **Collect Preferences**: Ask about dietary restrictions and special needs
- **Keep Updated**: Use messages to share any changes

### Communication
- **Be Clear**: Provide all necessary information upfront
- **Stay Responsive**: Answer guest questions promptly
- **Share Updates**: Keep everyone informed of any changes
- **Use Photos**: Share pictures to build excitement

## 🆘 Need Help?

### Common Issues
- **Can't Sign Up**: Check your internet connection and try again
- **Forgot Password**: Use "Forgot Password" on the sign-in screen
- **Can't Find Event**: Check your Events tab or search for it
- **Guests Not Receiving Invites**: Verify email addresses are correct

### Getting Support
- Check the app's help section
- Contact support through Settings → Help & Support
- Visit our website for additional resources
