# KinshipSync - Android User Guide: From Sign-Up to Your First Event

Welcome to KinshipSync for Android! This guide will walk you through creating your account and planning your first event on your Android device.

## 📱 Getting Started on Android

### Step 1: Download and Install
1. Open **Google Play Store** on your Android device
2. Search for **"KinshipSync"** or **"kinship sync"**
3. Tap **"Install"** to download the app
4. Wait for installation to complete
5. Tap **"Open"** to launch KinshipSync

### Step 2: First Launch & Permissions
When you first open the app, you'll see the welcome screen with KinshipSync branding. The app may request these permissions:

- **Camera Access**: For taking profile photos and sharing images in chats
- **Photo Library Access**: For uploading existing photos as profile pictures
- **Notifications**: To receive event updates and messages

**💡 Tip**: Grant these permissions for the best experience. You can change them later in Android Settings.

### Step 3: Create Your Account

#### Option A: Email Sign-Up (Recommended)
1. **Tap "Sign Up"** on the welcome screen
2. **Fill in Required Information:**
   - **Full Name** (required): Enter your first and last name
   - **Email Address** (required): Use a valid email you check regularly
   - **Password** (required): Create a strong password (at least 8 characters)

3. **Optional Information:**
   - **Phone Number**: Add your phone number for better communication
   - **Location**: Enter your city/country for location-based features
   - **Profile Picture**: Tap the camera icon to add a profile photo
     - Choose "Camera" to take a new photo
     - Choose "Gallery" to select an existing photo

4. **Accept Terms:**
   - Check the box to accept Terms & Conditions
   - Tap **"SIGN UP"** to create your account

#### Option B: Google Sign-In (Android Optimized)
1. Tap the **"Google"** button
2. Select your Google account from the list
3. Grant permissions when prompted
4. Your account will be created automatically with your Google profile info

**Note**: Apple Sign-In is not available on Android devices.

### Step 4: Welcome to Your Dashboard
After successful sign-up, you'll be taken to your personalized dashboard featuring:
- **Welcome Message**: Personalized greeting with your name
- **Upcoming Events**: Quick view of your next events (empty for new users)
- **Recent Activities**: Latest notifications and updates
- **Search Bar**: Find events quickly
- **Notification Bell**: See unread notification count

## 🎉 Creating Your First Event

### Step 1: Access Event Creation
1. From your dashboard, tap the **"Events"** tab at the bottom
2. Tap the **"Add new event"** button (blue button with + icon)

### Step 2: Event Basic Information
Fill in the essential details for your event:

1. **Event Name** (required)
   - Tap the text field and enter a clear, descriptive name
   - Example: "Smith Family Reunion 2024"

2. **Event Date** (required)
   - Tap the date field to open Android's date picker
   - Scroll to select your event date
   - Tap **"OK"** to confirm

3. **Event Time** (optional)
   - Tap the time field to open Android's time picker
   - Use the scroll wheels to select hours and minutes
   - Choose AM/PM
   - Tap **"OK"** to confirm

4. **Location** (optional)
   - Tap the location field and enter the venue or address
   - Example: "Central Park, New York" or "123 Main St, Anytown"

5. **Description** (optional)
   - Add details about your event
   - Include what guests should expect, dress code, etc.

6. **Visibility Settings**
   - **Private**: Only invited guests can see the event
   - **Public**: Anyone can find and join the event

7. **Invite Guests** (optional)
   - Tap **"Select Users"** to invite specific people
   - Choose from your contacts or app users
   - You can skip this and invite people later

8. Tap **"Next"** to continue

### Step 3: Choose Event Theme
1. **Browse Available Themes**
   - Scroll vertically through the theme options
   - Each theme has a unique color scheme and style

2. **Select Your Theme**
   - Tap on a theme to preview it
   - Choose the one that best fits your event
   - Tap **"Next"** to continue

### Step 4: Create Event Website (Optional)
1. **Website Details**
   - The app will auto-generate a website for your event
   - You can customize the content and appearance

2. **Review Website**
   - Check how your event website will look
   - Make any necessary adjustments

3. Tap **"Create Event"** to finalize

### Step 5: Event Created Successfully!
🎉 Congratulations! Your event has been created. You'll now see:
- A success message confirming your event
- Your event listed in the Events tab
- Options to manage your event

## 🆘 Android-Specific Troubleshooting

### Common Issues
- **App Won't Open**: Restart your device and try again
- **Permission Denied**: Go to Settings → Apps → KinshipSync → Permissions
- **Notifications Not Working**: Check notification settings in Android Settings
- **Slow Performance**: Clear app cache in Settings → Apps → KinshipSync → Storage

### Android Settings
- **App Permissions**: Settings → Apps → KinshipSync → Permissions
- **Battery Optimization**: Settings → Battery → Battery Optimization
- **Notifications**: Settings → Apps → KinshipSync → Notifications
